chapter one · the soft one

Honey 🍯

Honey is the name that feels soft. The kind of name that somehow makes an ordinary conversation feel a little warmer.

There are so many little moments that don't look important from the outside, but somehow stay in the heart. That's what this name reminds me of — the quiet, sweet parts of us.

Some people become special through one huge moment. Sometimes it's just a hundred tiny moments adding up until you suddenly realise: this person matters so much. 🥹
chapter two · the baby one

Baby 🧸

Then there's Baby — because apparently one nickname was never enough. 😂

It's the name for the clingy, silly, soft, annoying, adorable version of us that probably makes absolutely no sense to anyone else.

You somehow became the person who can make me smile at the most random times. Very inconvenient. Very cute. Would still complain about it again. 😂❤️
chapter three · the favourite

Baby Doll 🎀

This one is for the moments when everything feels extra soft — when a normal day suddenly feels special just because you're part of it.

It's funny how a person can become part of your everyday thoughts without even trying.

If memories had a favourite place to live, I think some of mine would choose the moments connected to you. Not because every moment was perfect — but because they were ours. 🤍
chapter four · the impossible one

Kuchupuchu 🐣

And then there is Kuchupuchu. 😭

A name that sounds completely ridiculous and somehow became completely normal.

Honestly, this is proof that love makes people do strange things — including inventing names that probably shouldn't exist. 😂

No dictionary has this word. No professor can explain it. No scientist can measure it. But somehow it means: you. That's enough. 😂❤️
chapter five · the real part

Us. 🤍

Of course, we're not a perfect little movie where everything is always sweet.

We fight. We misunderstand each other. Sometimes we're stubborn. Sometimes one of us gets upset and the other doesn't know what to say.

But after all the noise settles down, there is still something that matters: we care.

The arguments don't erase the good moments, and the difficult days don't erase the memories that made us smile.

chapter six · after the fight

Still you. 🥹

You can be annoyed with someone and still miss them. You can need space and still care. You can have a difficult conversation and later remember why the person matters.

Not because everything is perfect — but because the connection is important enough to make you want to understand each other and find your way back to calm.

The goal isn't to never fight. It's to keep choosing kindness, communication and understanding when things get difficult. That's the part worth protecting. 🤍
